Privacy policy

hermes-rclone is used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: files in the owner's own Google Drive, only to keep them synchronized with the owner's own server.
  • Use: the files are copied between Google Drive and that server. Nothing else is done with them.
  • Sharing: no data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training by this tool.
  • Storage: the access token is stored only on the owner's server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
